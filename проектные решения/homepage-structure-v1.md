# Homepage Structure — Восток-Агрохим

Version: 1.0

Status:
Approved concept

Purpose:
This document defines the approved structure and logic of the homepage.

The homepage should guide the visitor through the following sequence:

1. Trust
2. Understanding of company approach
3. Finding the right solution
4. Product selection
5. Contact

The website should not behave like a simple online catalog.
The main goal is to present the company as a reliable expert partner.

---

# 1. Hero Section

## Purpose

Create first impression and establish trust within the first seconds.

The visitor should understand:

- what the company does;
- who the company works with;
- why the company can be trusted;
- what action to take.

---

## Main message

Надёжные решения для защиты урожая.

Alternative approved direction:

Подбираем решения, а не просто продаём препараты.

---

## Supporting message

Средства защиты растений для сельхозпроизводителей.
Подбираем решения с учетом задач вашего хозяйства.

---

## CTA

Primary:

Получить консультацию

Secondary:

Смотреть каталог

---

## Visual direction

Concept:

Экспертность + аграрная тематика.

Preferred:

Человек + продукция.

Requirements:

- real working environment;
- no mandatory portrait;
- director's face is not used without approval;
- person can be shown from the back or without identification.

Avoid:

- generic agricultural stock photos;
- tractor as the main image;
- artificial corporate portraits.

---

# 2. How We Work Section

## Purpose

Show the difference between a consultant supplier and a simple seller.

Main idea:

Подбираем решения, а не просто продаём препараты.

---

## Structure

Three steps:

## 01. Понять задачу

We consider:

- crop specifics;
- existing problem;
- farm conditions;
- application requirements.

---

## 02. Подобрать решение

We help choose:

- suitable crop protection products;
- application approach;
- optimal solution for the situation.

---

## 03. Поставить продукцию

We provide:

- reliable supply;
- products from trusted manufacturers;
- convenient cooperation.

---

# 3. Solutions by Customer Needs Section

## Purpose

Navigation should be based on customer problems, not only product categories.

---

## Main message

Решения для разных задач хозяйства.

---

## Categories

- Защита от сорняков
- Защита от болезней
- Защита от вредителей
- Решения для защиты культур
- Подготовка к уборке

---

## UX principle

The visitor should first think:

"Мне нужно решить проблему"

not:

"Мне нужно найти название препарата"

---

# 4. Popular Solutions Section

## Purpose

Demonstrate available products without turning the website into an online store.

---

## Main message

Востребованные решения для хозяйств.

---

## Product card structure

Each card contains:

- product image;
- product name;
- manufacturer;
- purpose;
- link to details.

---

## Avoid

Do not emphasize:

- price as the main factor;
- aggressive sales;
- "buy now" approach.

The website should support consultation-based B2B sales.

---

# 5. Why Customers Trust Us Section

## Purpose

Show the competitive advantages of a small regional company.

---

## Main message

Работаем как партнер, а не просто как поставщик.

---

## Key points

## 01. Знаем продукт

Practical expertise and understanding of crop protection products.

---

## 02. Подбираем под задачу

Solutions are selected according to customer needs.

---

## 03. Проверенные производители

Reliable suppliers and original products.

---

## 04. Всегда на связи

Direct communication and customer support.

---

# General Homepage Principles

The homepage should communicate:

- reliability;
- expertise;
- accessibility;
- personal responsibility.

The homepage should NOT communicate:

- large anonymous corporation;
- online store only;
- aggressive sales;
- unrealistic market leadership.

Core customer feeling:

"Этой компании можно доверить задачу защиты урожая. Здесь помогут подобрать правильное решение."
