# Design Handoff — Восток-Агрохим

Version: 1.0

Status:
Approved design direction

## Project goal

Create a modern B2B website for a regional crop protection supplier.

The website should communicate:

- expertise;
- reliability;
- personal involvement;
- practical understanding of agricultural tasks.

---

## Approved visual direction

Brand direction:

"Уверенная классика"

Characteristics:

- restrained;
- professional;
- trustworthy;
- no visual tricks.

---

## Key positioning

The company is not presented as:

- a large anonymous distributor;
- a marketplace;
- an official representative without confirmation.

The company is presented as:

An expert supplier that helps agricultural businesses choose suitable crop protection solutions.

---

## Hero

Approved concept:

Person + product + working environment.

No director portrait.

Main message:

"Решения для защиты урожая, подобранные под задачи вашего хозяйства."

---

## Design restrictions

Avoid:

- unsupported partnership claims;
- manufacturer logo walls;
- aggressive sales style;
- excessive emphasis on price;
- generic stock agriculture imagery.

---

## Main page structure

Use:

1. Hero
2. Solution approach
3. Solutions by customer tasks
4. Product assortment
5. Company expertise
6. Manufacturers
7. Final CTA
